# Contact

Write to Vojta Holik at [vojta@holik.dev](mailto:vojta@holik.dev).

Good reasons to write:

- A technique is wrong, out of date, or breaks in a browser. Say which entry, which browser and which version.
- A specimen does not show what its check says it should.
- A credit is missing, or points at the wrong person or article.
- You know a technique that belongs on the list, or a reason one should come off it.

There is no form and no issue tracker. An email with the entry's number and what you saw is enough.

To read the list first, start at [good-css](/). To learn what this site is, see [About](/about). To learn what it does with your data, see [Privacy](/privacy).
