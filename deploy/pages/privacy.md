# Privacy

This site does not ask who you are and does not track what you do.

- It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
- It runs no analytics and no advertising or tracking script.
- It has no accounts. Some specimens show form fields to demonstrate a technique. What you type into them is not collected or stored.

Two other parties receive a request when you load a page:

- Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details with each request. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- The typefaces Barlow and Bebas Neue load from Google Fonts, so your browser also sends a request to Google. See [how Google Fonts handles data](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

If you write to the address on the [Contact](/contact) page, your message and your address are used to reply to you and for nothing else.

This page describes the site as of 1 October 2026.
