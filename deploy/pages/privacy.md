# Privacy

This site does not ask who you are and does not follow you across the web.

- It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.
- It counts page views with [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy), which sets no cookies and keeps no data that identifies you. It runs no advertising or tracking script.
- It has no accounts. Some specimens show form fields to demonstrate a technique. What you type into them is not collected or stored.

Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details with each request. See [Vercel's privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). The fonts are served from the site itself, so no font service gets a request.

If you write to the address on the [Contact](/contact) page, your message and your address are used to reply to you and for nothing else.

This page describes the site as of 3 November 2026.
